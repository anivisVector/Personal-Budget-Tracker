Expected format

The first row must be a header. date, type, amount and category are required; title and description are optional. This matches the Export file, so exports round-trip.

date,type,amount,category,title,description

  • Dates use YYYY-MM-DD; amounts are positive numbers.

  • Every row is validated; problems are listed by row number.
